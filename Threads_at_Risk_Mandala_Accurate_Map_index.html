Why this archive exists

About the archive

Threads at Risk is a digital archive that focuses on traditional Indian textile practices that are declining, changing, or at risk of being lost. The idea for this archive is inspired by UNESCO's initiative, Handmade for the 21st Century: Safeguarding Traditional Indian Textiles, which highlights the importance of protecting and promoting India's rich textile heritage.

The archive brings together textiles from different parts of India and documents their materials, techniques, motifs, communities, history, and present-day status.

Rather than looking at these textiles only as beautiful objects this archive also explores how and why these traditions have changed over time. Factors such as the decline of traditional patronage, industrial production, changing markets, and consumer preferences have affected many of these practices. Threads at Risk aims to preserve information about these textile traditions and make it accessible to students, researchers, and anyone interested in India's textile heritage.
